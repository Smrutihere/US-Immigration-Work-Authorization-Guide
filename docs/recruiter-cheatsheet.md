# Recruiter Quick Reference Guide

## Work Authorization Summary

| Status | Can Work? | Sponsorship Needed? |
|----------|----------|----------|
| US Citizen | Yes | No |
| Green Card Holder | Yes | No |
| H-1B | Yes | Transfer may be needed |
| OPT | Yes | Future sponsorship likely |
| STEM OPT | Yes | Future sponsorship likely |
| H-4 EAD | Yes | Depends on future plans |

## Questions to Ask Every Candidate

1. Are you legally authorized to work in the US?
2. Do you require sponsorship now?
3. Will you require sponsorship in the future?
4. What is your current visa status?
5. What is your work authorization expiration date?
