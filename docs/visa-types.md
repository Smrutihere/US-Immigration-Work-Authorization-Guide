# US Visa Types for Recruiters

## H-1B
- Employer-sponsored work visa
- Initial validity: 3 years
- Extendable up to 6 years

## F-1 OPT
- Work authorization for international students
- Usually valid for 12 months

## STEM OPT
- Additional 24-month extension
- STEM degree required

## TN Visa
- For Canadian and Mexican citizens
- Available under USMCA

## E-3 Visa
- For Australian citizens
- Employer-sponsored

## L-1 Visa
- Intra-company transfer visa
- For employees transferring from overseas offices

## H-4 EAD
- Work authorization for eligible H-4 dependents

## Green Card
- Permanent residency
- No employer sponsorship required after obtaining it
