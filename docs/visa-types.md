# US Visa Types for Recruiters

## H-1B
- Employer-sponsored work visa
- Initial validity: 3 years
- Extendable up to 6 years

## F-1 OPT
- Work authorization for international students
- Usually valid for 12 months

## STEM OPT
- Additional 24-month extension
- STEM degree required

## TN Visa
- For Canadian and Mexican citizens
- Available under USMCA

## E-3 Visa
- For Australian citizens
- Employer-sponsored

## L-1 Visa
- Intra-company transfer visa
- For employees transferring from overseas offices

## H-4 EAD
- Work authorization for eligible H-4 dependents

## Green Card
- Permanent residency
- No employer sponsorship required after obtaining it

## Quick Recruiter Notes

### No Sponsorship Required
- US Citizen
- Green Card Holder

### Temporary Work Authorization
- OPT
- STEM OPT
- H-4 EAD

### Employer-Sponsored Visas
- H-1B
- TN
- E-3
- L-1

### Important Screening Questions

1. Are you authorized to work in the US?
2. Do you require sponsorship now?
3. Will you require sponsorship in the future?
4. What is your current visa status?
5. What is your work authorization expiration date?
