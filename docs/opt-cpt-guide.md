# OPT & CPT Guide

## What is OPT?

Optional Practical Training (OPT) allows F-1 students to work in the US in a field related to their degree.

### OPT Duration
- 12 months standard OPT
- STEM graduates may qualify for 24-month extension

## What is CPT?

Curricular Practical Training (CPT) allows students to work while enrolled in school.

### CPT Types
- Part-Time CPT
- Full-Time CPT

## Recruiter Questions

1. Are you on OPT or STEM OPT?
2. What is your OPT expiration date?
3. Are you eligible for STEM extension?
4. Will you require sponsorship in the future?

## Common Documents

- EAD Card
- I-20
- Passport
- Visa Stamp
