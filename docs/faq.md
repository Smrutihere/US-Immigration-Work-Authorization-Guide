# Frequently Asked Questions

## Does an H-1B candidate need sponsorship?

Usually they need an H-1B transfer when changing employers.

## Can an OPT candidate work immediately?

Yes, if they have a valid EAD and work authorization.

## Can Green Card holders work without sponsorship?

Yes.

## What is STEM OPT?

A 24-month extension available to eligible STEM graduates.

## What is H-4 EAD?

Employment authorization granted to certain H-4 dependents.

## Should recruiters ask about visa status?

Yes, recruiters can ask about work authorization and sponsorship requirements.
