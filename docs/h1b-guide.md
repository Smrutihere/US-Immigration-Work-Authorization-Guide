# H-1B Recruiter Guide

## What is H-1B?

The H-1B visa allows US employers to hire foreign workers in specialty occupations.

## Key Facts

- Employer-sponsored
- Usually issued through the annual H-1B lottery
- Initial validity: 3 years
- Maximum stay: 6 years (with some exceptions)

## Recruiter Questions

1. Do you currently hold H-1B status?
2. Do you need an H-1B transfer?
3. What is your current visa expiration date?
4. Do you have an approved I-140?

## Common Terms

- H-1B Transfer
- Cap-Exempt
- I-140
- RFE
- Premium Processing
